# Notices for the built-in icons

`BuilderIcon.vue` draws the built-in icons of the Builder, from the
drawings in `src/builder/iconPaths.js`. Most of the drawings are original
to phenix. This file names each drawing that comes from another work, its
license and the changes to it. It also gives the credits and the trademark
statements that the icons need.

Names and logos of products are trademarks of their owners. phenix is not
affiliated with or endorsed by them.

## Original drawings

All other built-in icons are original drawings of phenix. These icons do
not show a logo:

- `apple` (Apple) shows the command key symbol (⌘, Unicode U+2318, the
  place of interest sign), not the Apple logo.
- `redhat` (Red Hat) shows a plain fedora hat, not the Red Hat logo.
- `windows` (Windows) shows a plain window with four panes, not the
  Windows logo.
- `centos` (CentOS) shows a four-part pinwheel, not the CentOS logo. The
  CentOS trademark guidelines allow no change to the logo other than
  scale, and the logo has four colors. The Builder draws each icon in one
  color, at sizes down to 16 pixels.
- `rtac` (RTAC) and `relay` (Relay) show generic front panels. They show
  no marks, model numbers or artwork of a manufacturer.
- `router` (Router) is an original drawing of the round router symbol of
  network diagrams. It is not a copy of the artwork of a manufacturer.
- `ubuntu` (Ubuntu) shows a badge with three dots on a ring. It is not
  the Circle of Friends logo.
- `fedora` (Fedora) shows a plain lowercase "f" in a square speech bubble.
  It is not the Fedora logo.
- `opensuse` (openSUSE) shows a generic chameleon from the side. It is not
  the Geeko logo.
- `alpine` (Alpine) shows a mountain peak with a line of snow. It is not
  the Alpine Linux logo.
- `kali` (Kali Linux) shows a generic dragon head. It is not the Kali
  dragon.
- `bsd` (BSD) shows a small horned daemon with a trident. It is not the
  BSD Daemon (Beastie) or the logo of a BSD project.
- `pfsense` (pfSense) shows a shield with a filter funnel. It is not the
  pfSense logo.
- `zeek` (Zeek) shows an eye on a network line. It is not the Zeek logo.
- `ntp` (NTP Server), `hacker` (Hacker), `lizard` (Lizard) and `fox` (Fox)
  are generic drawings.
